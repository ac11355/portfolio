About Azelin Chen

Hello. This is Azelin.

I'm a multi-media designer currently seeking a degree in Integrated Design and Media (B.S.) at the Tandon School of Engineering at New York University.

I design through dance, shapes, camera lenses, audio, textiles...

I have many passions and skills with many stories leading to them. But because this is my portfolio website, I'll vaguely share one relevant story:

While my parents sent me to guitar lessons and dance classes to make me more well-rounded, I only began to take charge of my creativity when I started to immerse myself in K-pop media during my teenage years. It is the space where I first learned Photoshop, video-editing, choreography, and even started my own trade business.
